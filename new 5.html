1.1 Introduction to Creation Ethics The ethical landscape of the Old Testament does not begin with the Law at Sinai, but with the Breath of God in Genesis.

Creation Ethics establishes the "Original Moral Order" the baseline for how humans ought to relate to God, to one another, and to the environment. This order suggests that morality is not an arbitrary set of rules imposed later in history, but is woven into the very fabric of existence. By understanding the origins of life, we understand the purpose of our moral conduct.
